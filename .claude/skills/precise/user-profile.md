## style preference: analytical/structured

- prefers named patterns and categorized observations over raw imagery or bare facts
- comfortable with technical/academic register
- wants explanatory framing — not just what, but what kind and what pattern
- metaphor tolerance: low-to-moderate (structural metaphors ok, decorative ones not)
- abstraction level: medium-high (subcategory-level, not instance-level unless warranted)
- terseness: moderate (concise but not telegraphic — sentences carry classificatory weight)
