## performance summary

**overall lift:** consistent ~2-3x across all metrics, with self-check (+84pp) and voice naturalness (+4.7) as the standout wins. the skill transforms AI-sounding listicles into writing that reads like it came from someone who actually understands the subject.

**best improvement:** sentence self-check (10% → 94%). baselines are almost entirely composed of sentences that could describe a different subject. skill outputs anchor to specific, non-transferable details. this is the core precision win.

**worst/regression:** precision_tool_technical — only +2 across most metrics. technical specifications already have low vagueness, so the skill has less room to operate.

**biggest gap remaining:** growth blurb self-check is only 50% — the skill trades precision for punchiness in short-form copy. voice naturalness for technical descriptions (5/10) suggests the skill still defaults to clinical tone when describing systems. no cross-lingual test cases in the suite, so that capability is untested.

**pattern:** the skill's primary mechanism is replacing adjectives with evidence. instead of saying someone is "intellectually curious," it names what they built. this works best when there's rich source material (todo lists, linkedin profiles, conversation transcripts) and worst when the input is already abstract (technical specs).
